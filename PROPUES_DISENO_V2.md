# 📔 UX Specification: "The Reliable Transition" Portfolio

**Project:** Michel Macias Cybersecurity Portfolio V2
**Concept:** Bitácora de Transición (Transition Log)
**Target Audience:** Technical Recruiters & SOC Managers looking for reliable, mature junior profiles.

---

## 1. User Flow & Information Architecture

The flow is designed to build trust by showing the evolution from a management background to a technical security role.

### User Journey
1.  **Entry (Hero):** Immediate recognition of the "Reliability + Transition" value proposition.
2.  **The Narrative (Bitácora):** Understanding the "Why" and "How" of the career change.
3.  **The Bridge (Transferable Skills):** Validating that "Junior Technical" does not mean "Inexperienced Professional."
4.  **The Proof (Learning Labs):** Seeing the technical rigor and methodology in action.
5.  **The Contact:** Clear call to action for professional engagement.

## 2. Screen-by-Screen Specifications

### A. Hero Section: "The Professional Pivot"
*   **Headline:** `Michel Macias`
*   **Sub-headline:** `Reliability forged in Management, applied to Cybersecurity.`
*   **Tagline:** `Junior Technical Profile | Senior Management Background | Transitioning with Rigor.`
*   **Visuals:** A clean, high-contrast layout. Use a "Status Badge" that says `[SYSTEM_STATUS: EVOLVING]`.
*   **Interaction:** Subtle parallax on the background grid.

### B. Section: Bitácora de Transición (Transition Log)
*   **Layout:** Vertical timeline (Left-aligned for mobile, alternating for desktop).
*   **Content Milestones:**
    1.  **The Foundation (Hospitality/Events):** "Managing high-pressure environments and complex logistics."
    2.  **The Spark (Interest in Security):** "Identifying the need for digital safety in physical operations."
    3.  **The Pivot (Education/Labs):** "Formalizing technical skills through active learning."
    4.  **The Present (Junior Security):** "Applying management rigor to technical challenges."
*   **Interaction:** Milestones "activate" (glow) as the user scrolls to them.

### C. Section: The Bridge (Transferable Skills)
*   **Layout:** A "Skill Mapping" grid.
*   **Mapping Examples:**
    *   `Crisis Management (Events)` ➔ `Incident Response Coordination`
    *   `Logistics & Planning` ➔ `Security Operations (SecOps)`
    *   `Team Leadership` ➔ `SOC Collaboration & Reporting`
    *   `Client Relations` ➔ `Stakeholder Communication in Security`
*   **Visual:** Use "Bridge" icons or arrows connecting the two worlds.

### D. Section: Active Learning Labs (Projects)
*   **Layout:** Card grid with a "Laboratory" aesthetic.
*   **Card Anatomy:**
    *   `Header`: Project Title + Status Badge (e.g., `[LAB_STATUS: DOCUMENTED]`).
    *   `Objective`: "To understand [X] vulnerability by simulating [Y] environment."
    *   `Methodology`: "Step-by-step investigation using [Tools]."
    *   `Key Learning`: "The most important takeaway from this lab."
*   **Interaction:** "View Lab Report" opens a clean Markdown viewer (already partially implemented).

## 3. Interaction & Visual Specifications

### Typography
*   **Headings:** `Space Grotesk` (Modern, technical).
*   **Body:** `Inter` (Clean, professional).
*   **Technical Notes:** `JetBrains Mono` (Honest, "under the hood" feel).

### Color Palette (The "Reliability" Palette)
*   **Primary (Background):** `#0A0F1E` (Deep Navy - Trustworthy).
*   **Secondary (Accent):** `#38BDF8` (Sky Blue - Technical/Modern).
*   **Alert/Action:** `#F59E0B` (Amber - Attention/Rigor).
*   **Text:** `#F8FAFC` (Off-white - High legibility).

### Micro-interactions
*   **The "Log" Effect:** When hovering over a project card, a small "timestamp" or "log entry" appears in the corner.
*   **Progressive Disclosure:** Use "Read More" for deep technical details to keep the main view clean and focused on the "Management" overview.

## 4. Usability Evaluation (Heuristic Check)

1.  **Visibility of System Status:** Use the `[SYSTEM_STATUS]` badges to show the portfolio is a "living document."
2.  **Match between System and Real World:** Use management terminology (Logistics, Crisis, Stakeholders) to bridge the gap for non-technical recruiters.
3.  **Consistency and Standards:** Maintain the "Log" aesthetic throughout all sections.
4.  **Aesthetic and Minimalist Design:** Avoid "hacker" clichés (green falling rain). Focus on "Professional Security Analyst" aesthetics.
